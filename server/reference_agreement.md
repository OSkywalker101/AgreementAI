3.	DURATION & FINANCIALS: 
The lease shall commence on or from 15/09/2020 and shall be in force initially for a period of 11 months and renewable thereafter with mutual understanding; subject to there being no breach of any of the terms and conditions herein. The Lessor and the Lessee shall renew the lease after expiry of the term for further two terms of 11 months on mutually agreed terms and conditions. However, neither the Lessee nor the Lessor can terminate the agreement till the expiry of 6 (six) months after the Lease comes into force. If such a termination occurs, an amount equivalent of 3 month’s rent shall be paid as penalty by whichever party terminates the agreement.

The Lessee shall be permitted to use the community common amenities such as swimming pool, club house, play areas, and other common facilities subject to the reasonable permissible limits and regulations set by Mantri Tranquil Apartment Owners Association.

8.  SUB-LEASE:

The Lessee shall not entitled or permitted to assign, underlet or sublet the schedule premises or any part thereof or part with possession of the demised property or any part thereof to anybody whatsoever.

9.   MAINTENANCE:

The Lessee shall keep the schedule premises in a fit and proper state subject to normal wear and tear by day-to-day maintenance including the fittings and fixtures etc, this includes the terrace area which the tenant has access to.
